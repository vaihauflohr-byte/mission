Formation de base

Course Content

FAST Basic Training is the second part of our three step training process aimed at helping people take in, live out, and then pass on the Word of God. It focuses in on the skills of discipleship, giving you nuts and bolts suggestions for how to study the Bible, pray, grow in obedience, share your faith, and more. Plus you will learn the master strategy behind the great commission, and how you can get involved!

This course consists of nine weeks of instruction. It includes weekly objectives, nine Bible searches, assigned memory verses for each study, illustrations, and many helpful practical suggestions.

  1. The Call to Commitment
  2. The Morning Watch
  3. The Promises of God
  4. Down to Business
  5. The Sword of the Spirit
  6. Personal Evangelism
  7. Bands of Believers
  8. The Mystery of Multiplication
  9. The Life of Purpose
