Tactiques d’équipe

Course Content

FAST Team Tactics is the third part of our three step training process aimed at helping people take in, live out, and then pass on the Word of God. It focuses in on the skills of discipleship, giving you nuts and bolts suggestions for how to study the Bible, pray, grow in obedience, share your faith, and more. Plus you will learn the master strategy behind the great commission, and how you can be a part!

This course consists of nine weeks of instruction. It includes weekly objectives, nine Bible searches, assigned memory verses for each study, illustrations, and many helpful practical suggestions.

  1. The Need of the Hour
  2. House to House
  3. Building Blocks
  4. Highways and Byways
  5. The Word of Life
  6. The Valley of Decision
  7. Preserving the Harvest
  8. The Call to Leadership
  9. Finishing the Work
